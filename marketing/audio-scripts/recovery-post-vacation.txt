Post-vacation sleep is uniquely hard. You had a week of late bedtimes, alcohol, time zone shifts, and complete schedule disruption. Now you are back, and your body has no idea what time it is. This protocol gets you back on track in three nights.

Here is what is misaligned. Your circadian clock has drifted by one to three hours depending on the vacation. Your sleep pressure is uneven because of naps and oversleeping. Your gut microbiome is recalibrating from changed food. Your nervous system was in low-stress mode and now is being asked to handle deadlines again. All of these affect sleep onset and quality.

The fix is three nights of strict reset.

Night one, the night you arrive home. Do not sleep in tomorrow, no matter how tired you are. Set your normal weekday alarm. Get sunlight within an hour of waking. This is the most important signal of the entire reset.

Night two. Caffeine cut-off is two PM today. No exceptions. Eat dinner at your usual time, even if you are not hungry. Move your body for at least thirty minutes outdoors. The fatigue from sunlight plus mild exercise primes deep sleep tonight.

Night three. Tonight should feel close to normal. Sleep onset is faster. Wake-ups are fewer. If you ran the protocol properly, by tomorrow morning the reset is complete.

What not to do. Do not nap on any of these three days, even briefly. Do not extend the weekend by sleeping in on day three. Do not have a glass of wine to help with sleep, even though you might want to.

Three days is enough. The body wants to be on a schedule. It just needs the signal.

Now, settle into this first night of the reset. The vacation is over. The rhythm is returning.

Breathe in for four seconds. Hold for seven. Release for eight.

Again. In for four. Hold for seven. Out for eight.

One last time. In for four. Hold for seven. Out for eight.

The clock starts now. Sleep well.
