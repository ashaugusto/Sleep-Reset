This is the Quick Reset. A two-night intensive protocol for when sleep has slipped and you cannot afford a full seven-night recalibration. This is the most aggressive version of CBT-I, and it works fast.

Here is the principle. The reason sleep slips is almost always that one or more of these three things broke. Your wake time anchor drifted. Your sleep window expanded too wide. Your bed became associated with wakefulness again. The Quick Reset fixes all three in forty-eight hours.

Tonight, night one. Compress your sleep window aggressively. Pick a wake time that is your earliest required wake time for the next month. Subtract six hours. That is your earliest bedtime tonight. You may feel awake at that time. You do not lie down before it. You may feel exhausted at nine PM. You do not lie down.

When you do lie down at the compressed bedtime, the fifteen minute rule applies strictly. If you are not asleep in fifteen minutes, get up, leave the bedroom, sit in low light without a screen, return only when sleepy. Repeat as needed. The first night feels long. That is correct.

Night two, tomorrow. Same wake time. Same compressed window. By tonight your body knows what is coming. Sleep onset will be substantially faster. Deep sleep will be longer. Wake-ups will be fewer.

By night three, you can expand the window by fifteen minutes, then keep expanding by fifteen minutes each subsequent night as long as you maintain at least eighty-five percent sleep efficiency.

What makes this work. The brain only learns sleep onset through the experience of fast sleep onset. By compressing the window, you guarantee that experience. Within forty-eight hours, the association rebuilds.

This is not a comfortable protocol. It is the fastest one.

Now, tonight, prepare for the compressed window. Wake time set. Bedroom cool. Phone in another room.

Breathe in for four seconds. Hold for seven. Release for eight.

Again. In for four. Hold for seven. Out for eight.

One last time. In for four. Hold for seven. Out for eight.

Forty-eight hours. Sleep returns.
