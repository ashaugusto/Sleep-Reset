Welcome to Night 3 of the Cognitive Shutdown Method. Tonight we focus on the fuel, what you put into your body during the day, and how it shapes the night.

Three substances matter the most. Caffeine has a half life of five to six hours in most adults. That means coffee at 2 PM still leaves half its dose circulating in your bloodstream at 8 PM. For sensitive people, that single dose can fragment sleep for the entire night.

Alcohol is more deceptive. It helps you fall asleep faster, but it suppresses REM sleep and fragments the second half of the night. You wake at three AM, dehydrated, with the alcohol metabolites pulling you out of deep sleep. The drink that felt relaxing at 9 PM is the same drink waking you at 3 AM.

Heavy meals close to bedtime force your digestive system to stay active when it should be powering down. The body cannot enter restorative sleep while it is still digesting.

So the rules for tonight are simple. No caffeine after 2 PM. No alcohol within three hours of bed. Last meal at least two hours before lying down. And drink most of your water in the first half of the day so your bladder does not wake you up.

Now, let your body settle. Loosen your jaw. Let your tongue rest behind your lower teeth. Let your shoulders fall away from your ears.

Breathe in through your nose for four seconds. Hold for seven seconds. Release slowly through your mouth for eight seconds.

Again. In for four. Hold for seven. Out for eight.

Once more. In for four. Hold for seven. Out for eight.

Your body has what it needs to rest. Sleep well.
