Welcome to Night 6 of the Cognitive Shutdown Method. Tonight we combine everything you have learned into a single bedtime protocol.

By now, your wake time is fixed. Your screens have stopped sixty minutes before bed. Caffeine, alcohol, and heavy meals are off the table for the evening. Your bedroom is cool, dark, and quiet. Your mind has been emptied onto paper.

Tonight is about sequence. Each element you have learned matters, but the order matters more. The right sequence creates momentum toward sleep. The wrong sequence breaks the chain.

Here is your protocol, starting ninety minutes before your wake time, minus eight hours, which gives your body the realistic sleep window.

Ninety minutes out. Constructive worry. Brain dump on paper, problems left on the desk.

Sixty minutes out. Screens off. Phone in another room. Switch to a book, stretching, or a warm shower.

Forty-five minutes out. Lower the lights in the room you are in. Dim, not bright. Use lamps, not overheads.

Fifteen minutes out. Brush teeth, bathroom, water by the bed. Final low-stimulation activity.

Zero. Into the bedroom, into the bed. Cool, dark, quiet. Eyes close.

That is it. Five steps. Same order every night. Your brain learns the sequence and prepares for sleep before you even lie down.

Now let your body remember the sequence. Soften your hands. Soften your eyes. Soften your jaw.

Breathe in slowly for four seconds. Hold gently for seven. Release through your mouth for eight.

Again. In for four. Hold for seven. Out for eight.

One last time. In for four. Hold for seven. Out for eight.

The protocol is yours. Sleep well.
