Welcome to Night 7, the final night of the Cognitive Shutdown Method. Tonight is about consolidation. Locking in what worked, and letting go of what did not.

Six nights ago, you started this protocol. You set an anchor. You stopped screens early. You changed what you fueled your body with. You shaped your environment. You emptied your mind. You learned the sequence.

Not all of it worked equally well for you. That is expected. Sleep is individual. Some interventions move the needle for you specifically, and some do less. The research is clear that the people who maintain results over years are the ones who identify their two or three highest-leverage habits and hold those rigid, while letting the others stay flexible.

So tonight, you make a choice. Of the six interventions, which three made the most difference for your sleep? Maybe it was the wake time anchor and the brain dump and the cool room. Maybe it was screens off and the constructive worry and the protocol sequence. Whatever it was, those three become permanent.

The others stay as tools you can return to when sleep slips, and sleep will slip occasionally. Travel, stress, illness. Those are normal. When it happens, you re-run the protocol. Two or three nights is usually enough to recalibrate.

You are not finished tonight. You are equipped. The protocol stays with you.
